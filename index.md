## Research Vision

My long-term research vision is to develop reliable, human-centered methods for modeling and improving complex decision systems. I am particularly interested in digital twins and AI-assisted scientific modeling for settings in which individual behavior, domain knowledge, and system-level constraints interact dynamically.

Three themes guide this vision: **1) Human-Centric AI.** Human behavior introduces heterogeneity, uncertainty, and causal complexity into decision systems. I aim to build models that capture how people reason, respond, and interact with AI-supported interventions. **2) Domain Knowledge.** Expert knowledge provides essential structure when data are limited or costly. I study how large language models and structured elicitation can represent and incorporate that knowledge efficiently. **3) Intelligent Decision Systems.** I develop simulation, machine-learning, and optimization methods that connect individual behavior with system-level outcomes and support transparent, robust decisions.
