My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, simulation, optimization, and data-driven decision-making, with an emphasis on translating rigorous models into reliable solutions for real-world systems.

## Research Vision

My long-term research vision is to develop reliable, human-centered methods for modeling and improving complex decision systems. I am particularly interested in digital twins and AI-assisted scientific modeling for settings in which individual behavior, domain knowledge, and system-level constraints interact dynamically.

Three themes guide this vision: **1) Human-Centric AI.** Human behavior introduces heterogeneity, uncertainty, and causal complexity into decision systems. I aim to build models that capture how people reason, respond, and interact with AI-supported interventions. **2) Domain Knowledge.** Expert knowledge provides essential structure when data are limited or costly. I study how large language models and structured elicitation can represent and incorporate that knowledge efficiently. **3) Intelligent Decision Systems.** I develop simulation, machine-learning, and optimization methods that connect individual behavior with system-level outcomes and support transparent, robust decisions.

## Research Accomplishments

My research spans multi-agent simulation, causal learning, Bayesian network structure learning, and uncertainty modeling. Across these areas, I combine statistical analysis, machine learning, mathematical modeling, and domain expertise to study complex systems from both individual and system-level perspectives.

### Multi-Agent Urban Emergency Evacuation Simulation

- Built a large-scale multi-agent evacuation simulation framework using OpenStreetMap-based urban spatial inputs and dynamic policy constraints.
- Developed an analysis pipeline combining correlation analysis, mutual information, and random-forest regression to capture linear and nonlinear dependencies.
- Identified threshold effects and behavioral phase shifts to support shelter placement and evacuation-guidance decisions.

### LLM-Enhanced Bayesian Network Structure Learning

- Reproduced and deployed the LLAMBO method with locally deployed large language models through Ollama.
- Integrated structured expert-knowledge elicitation into Bayesian optimization to reduce intervention costs and improve search efficiency.

### Causal Inference and LLM Uncertainty

- Designed expert questionnaires to capture human causal-reasoning patterns.
- Built and validated regression-based mathematical models of intervention cost and uncertainty behavior.
- Co-authored ongoing work on crowd-informed causal learning and learning causal graphs from human knowledge; see [Publications](/publications/) for details.
